A Personal Note on the New Direction of Travel Creations by Di

March 8, 2026 By Diane Mack · Owner & Luxury Travel Curator

Dear Travel Family (Present and Future),

As I sit down to reflect on the exciting shifts happening here at Travel Creations by Di, I wanted to share a thought with you about a word that gets tossed around a lot in our industry: Luxury.

When most people see that word, they immediately think "EXPENSIVE." They envision gold-leafed lobbies, five-figure nightly rates, and perhaps a level of travel that feels out of reach.

But when I say the word "Luxury," I see something entirely different.

To me, luxury isn't just about the physical location where you lay your head—it's about the standard of care you receive from the moment you begin dreaming of a getaway.

True luxury is the peace of mind that comes from being heard. It is having a professional who listens to your visions and dreams, then meticulously curates an experience that fits you perfectly. Whether you choose the most opulent suite on the ship or a cozy boutique hotel tucked away in the Texas Hill Country, my mission is to make you feel that luxury through the service I provide.

As a Curator of Travel, my goal is always to find the "optimum bang for your buck." I take pride in providing the best value for every traveler, every single time, regardless of the price tag.

However, because this level of personal curation requires a deep investment of time, expertise, and advocacy, I have to be clear about who we serve best. If you are looking for the "lowest possible deal" by price-matching quotes against an automated booking site, Travel Creations by Di might not be the right match for you.

We aren't a search engine; we are your partners in discovery. We are here for the travelers who value their time and want a vacation that feels hand-picked, vetted, and seamless.

We are shifting our focus to offer even more premium experiences, but that doesn't mean we aren't still here for everyone—wherever you are on your journey. We are simply committing to providing the most luxurious service possible to ensure your travel dreams come true.

Thank you for being part of this journey with me. I can't wait to curate your next adventure.

Sincerely,

Diane Mack

Owner & Luxury Travel Curator
